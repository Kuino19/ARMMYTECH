We're proud to introduce Armstrong & Ifedayo Tech LTD — a Nigerian technology company building innovative software products that help businesses, professionals, and communities across Africa operate more efficiently.

## Our Vision

At A&I Tech, our vision is simple: to make technology accessible and transformative for African enterprises. From compliance to operations, the right software can drastically reduce friction.

### Why We Started

*   **Complexity:** Regulatory compliance in Africa is often opaque.
*   **Access:** High quality enterprise software shouldn't be out of reach.

> "The future of Africa depends on the technology we build for it today."

We are incredibly excited about our flagship product **Kompli**, which is set to revolutionize legal compliance for businesses.
