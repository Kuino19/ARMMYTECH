At A&I Tech LTD, our mission has always been to innovate, build, and empower. Today, I am incredibly proud to announce a special initiative that brings that mission to life for the next generation. 

We are partnering with **Foursquare Gospel Church, Isheri** to host the **VBS Technology Track: AI & Robotics Fundamentals**. 

This is a 2-week, hands-on learning experience designed to introduce young minds (ages 8 to 16) to the exciting worlds of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics. But more than just technology, this program is built on the foundation of creativity, innovation, and faith.

## What the Students Will Explore

Over the course of two weeks, our young innovators will dive deep into:

*   **AI Basics:** Discovering the power and potential of Artificial Intelligence.
*   **Robotics:** Getting hands-on to build and program real robots.
*   **Coding:** Learning the fundamental logic and language behind the code.
*   **Sensors:** Understanding how machines interact with the world by sensing and responding.
*   **Problem Solving:** Developing the critical thinking skills needed to solve real-world challenges creatively.

## Faith + Technology

We believe that building young minds involves shaping both their intellect and their character. A unique aspect of this program is its focus on *Faith + Technology*. We are teaching these children to learn, build, and grow in their faith and purpose—ultimately using their skills to innovate for God's glory.

## Program Details

*   **Duration:** 2 Weeks
*   **Target Audience:** Children & Teens (Ages 8 – 16)
*   **Venue:** Foursquare Gospel Church, Isheri
*   **Certification:** A Certificate of Completion will be awarded to all participants.

Spaces for this track are limited! We want to raise a generation of innovators and problem solvers who are equipped for tomorrow. 

If you'd like to register your child or learn more, scan the QR code on the flyer or reach out to us at [hello@goanitech.com](mailto:hello@goanitech.com).
