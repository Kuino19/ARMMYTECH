Regulatory compliance is one of the biggest hidden costs for African businesses. Kompli was built to change that — here's how AI is making compliance accessible to everyone.

## The Problem

Navigating corporate law, tax filing, and data protection regulations can be daunting. Many startups struggle with finding accurate information and filing the necessary paperwork without spending exorbitant amounts on legal fees.

## Enter Kompli

Kompli is an AI-powered compliance platform designed specifically for the African context. It continuously monitors changing regulations and provides actionable, step-by-step guidance.

### Features
1. **Automated Reminders:** Never miss a filing deadline again.
2. **AI Legal Assistant:** Get plain-English answers to complex regulatory questions.
3. **Document Generation:** Automatically generate required legal templates.

We believe that compliance should be a tool for growth, not a barrier.
